# Payroll calculation and independent verification

The software is an auditable calculation system, not a certificate of Philippine payroll compliance. Every employee in every run needs an independent human verification record, even when deterministic software fixtures pass. Browsing and test-fixture approvals are not accounting sign-off.

## Supported scope and sources

The engine supports ordinary Philippine salaried employees, monthly and semimonthly periods, and PHP centavos. Authorized payroll staff must explicitly save effective statutory rules in Payroll → Settings. The unsaved reference draft comes from the historical `PH-2025-01-v1` source registry and has a 2026-12-31 software review boundary; this does not assert that an issuance expires then. Review superseding issuances and save an applicable configuration with a documented review end date. New generation never silently activates the template or falls back to application rates. Other jurisdictions, hourly/daily payroll, household workers, self-employed contributions, minimum-wage tax exemptions, annualized withholding, final pay, and statutory annual benefit exemptions require a reviewed extension or explicitly documented per-run corrections; do not assume this ordinary-employee model covers them.

Primary sources inspected on 2026-10-21:

- [SSS Circular 2024-006 and contribution schedule](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/2025-SSS-Contribution-Table-rev.pdf), linked from [SSS contribution resources](https://www.sss.gov.ph/pay-contribution/). The engine separates regular SS, MPF and employer-only EC and applies the schedule's salary-credit brackets.
- [PhilHealth Advisory 2025-0002](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2025/PA2025-0002.pdf); the [2026 advisories](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2026/archives.php) and [2026 circulars](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/circulars/2026/) were checked for updates. This source discovery does not replace independent confirmation of current applicability.
- [Pag-IBIG Circular 460 in an official DMW-hosted issuance](https://dmw.gov.ph/archives/v1/resources/dsms/DMW/ISN-EXT/2025/DMW-ADVISORY-37-2025.pdf). The issuing agency's [original PDF](https://www.pagibigfund.gov.ph/document/pdf/circulars/provident/Circular%20No.%20460%20-%20Guidelines%20on%20the%20Pag-IBIG%20Fund%27s%20Implementation%20of%20Increase%20in%20the%20MFS%20Effective%20February%202024.pdf) was blocked by its browser verification challenge. Confirm the original with the payroll reviewer.
- [BIR RR 11-2018 Annex E](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/Annex%20E%20RR%2011-2018.pdf), with separate monthly and semimonthly withholding brackets effective from 2023.

Statutory values are immutable, effective-dated workspace configuration. Settings provide structured fields for SSS/MPF/EC salary tables, Pag-IBIG thresholds/rates/caps, PhilHealth total premium and shares, BIR brackets, rounding, collection timing and documented legal exemptions. Only `hr.payroll.settings.manage` permits editing; titles and general administrator roles do not. Saving a new version invalidates affected open calculations and approvals, while closed periods reject backdated changes. Calculation snapshots include rule configuration, effective compensation, settings, employment, attendance, canonical approved leave/overtime, and explicitly classified adjustments.

## Arithmetic and precedence

All stored amounts are integer PHP centavos. Ratios use BigInt intermediates. Business earnings round half away from zero to the nearest centavo; each statutory component and withholding use the configured nearest/up/down centavo policy. PhilHealth rounds total premium before assigning the employee share and employer remainder. Salaried base pay is weighted by scheduled work minutes at each effective salary within the full period, divided by the configured frequency. Hire/end dates prorate eligible minutes. Monthly allowances use the latest effective profile and frequency; a reviewer must correct partial-period or mid-period allowance eligibility explicitly.

Unpaid leave and attendance deductions are separate lines. Attendance deductions follow the workspace's explicit policy. Paid leave reduces scheduled attendance obligations without being a payroll deduction. Extra logged minutes become payable only up to approved overtime. Holiday/rest-day premiums use configured business multipliers. Night differential uses local 22:00–06:00 minutes excluding recorded unpaid breaks; reviewed corrections with unspecified break intervals require explicit night-pay review and, when needed, a classified adjustment.

Contributions use each configuration’s explicit salary basis and collection policy. The reference draft uses actual monthly remuneration for SSS, basic salary plus allowances for Pag-IBIG, and monthly basic salary for PhilHealth. Actual-remuneration collection in semimonthly payroll reconciles the finalized first cutoff with the second cutoff and collects at month-end; missing source payroll blocks release unless employment starts in the second cutoff. Monthly-basic/compensation contributions can split across cutoffs, with odd centavos assigned to the second, or collect at month-end/final employment cutoff. Withholding uses the appropriate period's table after employee statutory contributions and taxable adjustments. Employer-only contributions do not reduce employee net pay. Non-taxable allowances, statutory adjustments, employment proration and unusual earned/paid-period treatment require reviewer evidence. Expenses/advances enter payroll only through a deliberate classified adjustment referencing an approved request. Purchase, travel and general approvals cannot be auto-imported as payroll deductions.

## Required review workflow

1. Configure business policies, reviewed statutory rules and effective compensation. Statutory versions start on day 1 of a month and one version must cover the whole payroll period. Review dates, rate fields, salary/credit limits, brackets and source links; confirm applicability and save explicitly. An editor cannot change their own compensation. Assign the reviewer `hr.payroll.read_all` and `hr.payroll.runs.approve`; assign operators `hr.payroll.runs.manage` separately.
2. Create the full monthly or semimonthly period and attendance cutoff. Resolve incomplete time entries, missing compensation/schedules and rule coverage warnings. Use documented, classified adjustments for payroll-specific corrections.
3. Calculate. Recalculation replaces the draft's employee snapshots and invalidates previous verification. Never edit a closed run.
4. A different authorized reviewer independently checks each employee's gross pay, paid/unpaid leave, approved time, earnings classifications, SSS/MPF/EC, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, withholding, caps, rounding, prior cutoff allocation and net pay against applicable official rules and accounting records. Review any employee-specific exemption, annual limit or exceptional treatment. If the engine's line differs, return to draft and enter a documented correction, then recalculate.
5. For each employee, the reviewer enters the independently determined net amount and evidence/source references, and explicitly confirms all lines and warnings were checked. The API requires matching net pay, current revision, a reviewer other than the calculator, and a reviewer other than the employee. This is the explicit manual-verification process required while rules remain unverified independently.
6. Approve only when every employee has that record and no blocking warnings remain. Finalize only after the period closes. Publishing is separate unless explicitly configured; employees cannot access drafts or unreleased payslips, including through exports or operator views of their own pay.
7. Record paid only after confirming payment outside Lumo. Lumo performs no transfers or government remittances.

Unpaid runs can be voided with a reason and reissued with an explicit replacement link. Paid runs require a documented subsequent-period correction. Closed-period leave/overtime/correction changes and backdated compensation/settings changes are blocked. A finalized first cutoff referenced by closed month-end reconciliation cannot be voided independently. Original snapshots, reviewer evidence and audit records remain intact.

No actual payroll/accounting reviewer has certified these rules as part of implementation. Production operators must follow the process above for every run; software test confirmations do not count as production approval.
